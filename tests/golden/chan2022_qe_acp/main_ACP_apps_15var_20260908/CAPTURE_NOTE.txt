This is the paper's 15-variable sign-restricted application: 1000 posterior draws
satisfying five sign restrictions and three row inequalities, found by rejection
sampling. It is the longest single run in the repository.

The model selector was left at the shipped dataset = 2; nothing else was patched.
The run was launched detached on 2026-09-01 and outlived two harness timeout
wrappers, which is why the log is the only artifact: main_ACP_apps.m saves no
.mat, it draws figures, so there is nothing else to capture. The acceptance
trajectory in the log is the substance - it is what makes the cost of this
identification scheme concrete, and it is the regression anchor for
replications/chan2022_qe_acp/run_all.m at dataset 2, which the unit test cannot
reach (test_acp_equivalence covers dataset 1 at nsim = 30).
